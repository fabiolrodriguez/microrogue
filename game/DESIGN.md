# The Last Vigil / A Última Vigília

A knight survives four 65-second seals and defeats the Warden in a single authored crypt. Target run: 5–7 minutes, including decisions and boss. No permanent progression.

- Hold sword attack: a generous arc with nearest-target assistance. Mouse attacks aim at the cursor. Roll through danger; rolls cannot pass through architecture. Keyboard and gamepad have equal targeting assistance.
- Four pillars break line of sight and split routes. Spikes alternate between horizontal and vertical lanes after seal two. All hostile arrivals, charges, bolts and traps have windups. Entry and reward space is the central dais.
- Rats pursue and swarm; goblins lock a direction before charging; skeletons maintain distance and fire blockable bolts; slimes occupy space and leave short-lived pools on death.
- Four seals last at least 65 seconds with spawn pulses ending at 55 seconds, followed by cleanup, a chest and one of three distinct relic choices. Recover one heart at each chest. Hearts also drop every ten kills.
- Eight unique relics, each available once, alter reach/damage, cadence, projectile reflection, sword waves, low-health power, roll frequency, maximum life or kill healing.
- Warden: alternating aimed fans, rotating radial gaps and telegraphed ground eruptions. At half health, faster patterns and limited rat support. Defeating it wins immediately.
- Game-specific simulation and feedback live in game/. Core autoloads remain unchanged. Arena rendering uses a nearest-filtered low-resolution viewport; native UI uses the Starter Kit theme, semantic palette and localization.
